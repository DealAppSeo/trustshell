TrustShell privacy

The reply text is sent to our checker, Groq. It is not stored. Not printed is not the same as not sent.

The extension reads the last assistant reply on chatgpt.com, chat.openai.com, claude.ai, gemini.google.com, grok.com, deepseek.com, and chat.deepseek.com.

That text is sent to https://repid-engine-production.up.railway.app/api/v1/classify. The answer is one word: pass, veto, or not-checked. A missing reply is not-checked.

The extension does not click, type, or send the chat.
