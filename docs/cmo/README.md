Bolt-on role pack. PAI keeps private memory. This pack does not.
Publish is a human grant. Drafts are the only default.

## "Face and table" means

You on camera. A grid on screen. Not a HeyGen avatar pretending to be you.

1. Phone or Screenity, vertical 9:16 (TikTok) or landscape for the LinkedIn document cousin.
2. First 3 seconds: your face **and** the four-row grid (see `FOUR-SECRETS.md`).
3. You point at each row and say where it goes. One breath per row.
4. Last 10 seconds: the gift (“screenshot this table”) and the duet prompt.
5. HeyGen is optional **after** that take, labeled AIGC, never the only face.

Episode 1 is the two hours you spent putting `sb_secret_` in `DATABASE_URL`.
