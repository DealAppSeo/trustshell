# Does a check name two model families? Production, 2026-10-23

The site, the glossary, `/mission` and `llms.txt` say a check is made by two model families. This is
the measurement that backs the part of that which can be measured. It is a dated record, not a live
check: run `node scripts/probe-families.mjs` for that.

Two calls to production (`https://repid-engine-production.up.railway.app`), no key, no account, made
at 2026-10-23T23:17Z, each with the same fixed public sentence, `Paris is the capital of France.`
The bodies as returned are in `fixtures/families-probe-2026-10-23.json`.

| Endpoint (what uses it) | What named the families | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| `POST /api/v1/classify` (CLI `check`, the extension, the home box) | `votes[].family` | `gpt-oss` (Groq) and `qwen` (Cerebras), both TRUE |
| `POST /api/v1/hal/evaluate`, strictness 2 (SDK `verify`, MCP `verify_output`) | `signals.families` | `qwen` and `openai`; `independent_hosts: 2`; a third checker (zai) came in late and was not counted |

Both endpoints named at least two different families. That is what "two model families" claims, and
on this date it is true.

## What this does and does not show

- It shows what production answered for one sentence, at one moment. It is not an accuracy figure and
  it does not say the families are the same two on every sentence: on 2026-10-16 the home card's own
  run had both families served by Groq (`docs/measurements/hero-card-2026-10-16.md`), and backups
  from other families take a turn when a primary cannot answer.
- It does **not** show the families are *independent* in the statistical sense. Different builders is
  not different training data, and nothing here measures how often they err together. The word
  "independent" in the copy is not something this can back; "two named model families" is.
- It does not show the families differ from the family of the model that wrote the thing being
  checked. Nothing excludes that today (audit F7).
- The two endpoints name the same lineage differently: classify says `gpt-oss`, HAL says `openai`.
  The probe reports them separately and never merges names across endpoints.
- The default voter pair in `repid-engine` is gpt-oss plus qwen only from `repid-engine#1462`, which
  is open and not on that repo's main. Production already had the Cerebras key, so its pair was
  already two families; without the key the old default would have been gpt-oss twice. That is the
  case the probe's FAILED branch exists to catch.
