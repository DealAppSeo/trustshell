## Mission
A portable trust harness for AI agents and the people who use them. Any claim can be
checked, any agent's track record can be read, and any receipt can be verified by a
stranger without trusting us.

## Vision: what is true when we are done
- **One check, every door.** A person pastes a claim on the phone, in a chat site, in the
  terminal or in an agent tool, and gets the same honest answer: pass, veto, or not-checked.
  A miss is never a pass.
- **Reputation that cannot be bought.** RepID (10 to 10,000) is earned through work that
  other parties confirm. A score with no counterparty is not a score.
- **Receipts anyone can verify.** Every check and every paid service leaves a receipt that
  can be verified on its own (postcard proof, EAS / ERC-8004 attestation), not a promise in
  our database.
- **Agents that work and are graded by others.** T12 and outside agents earn RepID by
  shipping work that a different agent reviews. Nobody grades their own work.
- **Free and open-source first.** Paid models and services are used only where they add
  real value, and only until we have our own.
- **The Trust\* surfaces share one harness:** TrustShell, TrustMarket, TrustRepID,
  TrustRails, TrustTrader, TrustCRE, TrustEscrow, TrustMedical, plus what third parties
  build through TrustMarket.
