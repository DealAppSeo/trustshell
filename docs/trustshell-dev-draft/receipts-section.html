Receipts, not promises.

A TrustShell action can seal a signed receipt of what it did. Verifying one offline tells you exactly what it proves, and it is strict about what it refuses to claim.

One receipt, six separate claims

There is no single “valid” badge. verify-receipt recomputes the hash and the signature locally and reports each claim on its own, with its own gaps.

$ trustshell verify-receipt r.json --root 0x8e8c…7222 exit 0
Offline verdict — sealer ✓ trusted under the pinned key. No network was used.
  • IntegrityVERIFIEDThe bytes are whole: the hash matches the contents, and a pinned Ed25519 key sealed them.
  • AuthenticityNOT_CHECKEDNo six-claim envelope was supplied, and this cannot be derived from the receipt alone.
  • ObservationNOT_CHECKEDNo outcome (e.g. a payment settling on chain) was observed and attested.
  • CorrectnessNOT_CHECKEDThe content was not verified against its source.
  • AuthorizationNOT_CHECKEDWhether the kernel permitted this action under its policy is not established here.
  • ContainmentNOT_CHECKEDThat the tool ran only under an ALLOW decision is not established here.
NOT_CHECKED is never a pass. Offline verification proves two things by cryptography: the bytes are whole, and a pinned key sealed them. Every other claim is the issuer’s own attestation, read but not re-derived, so it shows as NOT_CHECKED until an authenticated envelope backs it.

Three levels of evidence, never collapsed

A signature says who sealed a receipt, not that an action was permitted or that it earned a reputation. The levels do not blur together, and you can only reach the first one by yourself.

Owner-self-signed you can do this now

Sealed with your install’s own key, offline — trustshell demo or sign-receipt. Integrity verifies under the key you pin.

Proves authorship and wholeness, nothing more. Not independent verification, and it earns no production RepID.

Independently-verified the engine’s key

An engine key sealed the receipt and an authenticated six-claim envelope backs it, so at least one claim beyond Integrity reads VERIFIED.

Still not reputation. Verification and reputation are separate gates.

RepID-eligible the engine decides

Whether a receipt earns portable reputation is the engine’s call, against its own pinned key.

Never an offline verdict, and never self-declared. An owner cannot promote their own receipt here.

What a receipt does not prove

  • An owner self-signature proves authorship and wholeness — not that the engine permitted the action, and not that it earned reputation.
  • NOT_CHECKED is never rounded up to a pass. A claim with no evidence stays not-checked; there is no single rollup “valid”.
  • Offline verification is stateless. It does not catch a verbatim replay of one receipt. Replay is prevented by the engine’s receipt-id and nullifier, not by the offline verifier.
  • A pinned root is required to accept a seal. With no key pinned, a whole-but-unaccountable receipt is NOT_CHECKED, never exit 0.
